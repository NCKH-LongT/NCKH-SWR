# Abstract

Business Analysts (BAs) are increasingly expected to work alongside Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools, particularly Large Language Models (LLMs), in modern software development environments. As AI capabilities continue to expand, the responsibilities of BAs are evolving from performing routine analytical tasks toward supervising AI-assisted processes, validating outputs, and facilitating collaboration between business stakeholders and technical teams.

However, existing studies investigate this transformation from different perspectives, including Business Analyst competencies, Requirements Engineering, AI adoption, and labor market trends. Few studies synthesize these perspectives to explain what AI-related skills are becoming essential for Business Analysts and how these skills align with current industry expectations.

This study aims to identify the essential competencies required for Business Analysts in AI-enabled software development, examine how AI-related skills are reflected in industry job market research, and explore the gap between academic literature and practical workforce demands.

To achieve this objective, we conducted a structured literature review of selected academic publications covering Business Analysis, Requirements Engineering, Large Language Models, AI adoption, and AI-related labor market studies. The selected papers were analyzed comparatively based on their research context, methodology, key findings, and relevance to the research questions.

The review indicates that traditional Business Analyst competencies—including analytical thinking, stakeholder communication, and domain knowledge—remain fundamental, while AI literacy, prompt engineering, AI output evaluation, and human–AI collaboration are emerging competencies. The findings also suggest that industry increasingly values hybrid skill sets combining technical AI knowledge with communication and business understanding, whereas academic research continues to focus primarily on technical AI applications. These findings provide a synthesized view of emerging competency requirements and highlight opportunities for aligning Business Analyst education with evolving industry needs.

**Keywords:** Business Analyst, Artificial Intelligence, Large Language Models, Requirements Engineering, AI Skills
