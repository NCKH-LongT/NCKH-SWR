# Abstract

## Draft Abstract

In higher education, particularly in software engineering and IT courses, instructors face significant challenges in creating Course Learning Outcome (CLO)-aligned assessments at scale. While Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)-based Learning Management Systems have emerged as a technically promising solution, existing research focuses primarily on implementing and evaluating AI components rather than formally specifying and validating the requirements for such systems. This study conducts a literature review of seven papers on RAG-based quiz generation, CLO-aligned assessment design, personalized feedback, and LMS adoption in Vietnamese higher education, analyzing each through a requirements engineering lens to identify gaps in existing requirements specification and validation practices. The review finds that no prior work formally specifies the functional requirements for CLO selection, question tagging, and coverage tracking as a requirements artifact; that the instructor review and approval workflow exists in practice but its functional requirements have never been formally defined; and that a 35% feedback error rate documented in existing systems is attributable to the absence of formally specified non-functional requirements for feedback quality thresholds. Additionally, empirical evidence from the Vietnamese higher education context shows that key LMS adoption factors — output quality, ease of use, and workload reduction — have never been translated into non-functional requirements for AI-assisted systems. Across all reviewed papers, no single work proposes a requirements validation framework covering technical faithfulness, CLO alignment, and stakeholder acceptance simultaneously. These findings highlight a critical gap between AI research and real-world educational deployment, and provide a structured requirements foundation for future development of RAG-based LMS systems.

**Keywords:** requirements engineering, retrieval-augmented generation, learning management system, CLO alignment, quiz generation, personalized feedback, higher education Vietnam
