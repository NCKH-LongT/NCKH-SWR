# 1. Paper: Requirements Engineering for Artificial Intelligence Systems: A Systematic Mapping Study
- **Paper link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.10693
- **Paper name:** Requirements Engineering for Artificial Intelligence Systems: A Systematic Mapping Study
- **Authors:** Khlood Ahmad, Mohamed Abdelrazek, Chetan Arora, John Grundy, Muneera Bano
- **Source:** https://arxiv.org/
- **Real problem:** Traditional Requirements Engineering techniques do not adequately address the unique characteristics of AI systems, such as learning behavior, uncertainty, explainability, and data dependency
- **Why is this problem important:** Poorly specified requirements can lead to unreliable AI recommendations, ethical issues and lack of trust from users
- **Gap:** Most RE4AI research focuses on autonomous vehicles

# 2. Paper: Requirements Elicitation and Modelling of Artificial Intelligence Systems: An Empirical Study
- **Paper link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.06034
- **Paper name:** Requirements Elicitation and Modelling of Artificial Intelligence Systems: An Empirical Study
- **Authors:** Khlood Ahmad, Mohamed Abdelrazek, Chetan Arora, John Grundy, Muneera Bano
- **Source:**  https://arxiv.org/
- **Real problem:** Developers often focus heavily on AI algorithms while neglecting human-centered requirements and stakeholder needs.
- **Why is this problem important:** AI systems that ignore user needs often suffer from low adoption, poor usability, lack of trust and inaccurate recommendations
- **Gap:** Requirements changed frequently during development

# 3. Paper: AI-Powered Smart Study Planner: Enhancing Personalized Learning Through Intelligent Scheduling
- **Paper link:** https://www.researchgate.net/publication/391661197_AI-Powered_Smart_Study_Planner_Enhancing_Personalized_Learning_Through_Intelligent_Scheduling
- **Paper name:** AI-Powered Smart Study Planner: Enhancing Personalized Learning Through Intelligent Scheduling 
- **Authors:** Gagana Lingshetti, Sarthak Pawar, Manish Shinde, Hamja Sayyed
- **Source:** International Journal of Scientific Research in Science, Engineering and Technology (https://www.researchgate.net/)
- **Real problem:** Students struggle to manage study schedules, prioritize subjects, adapt plans to changing deadlines and maintain consistent study habits
- **Why is this problem important:** Poor study planning can lead to lower academic performance, missed deadlines, stress and burnout
- **Gap:** The paper mainly focuses on scheduling optimization and personalized learning but provides limited discussion about Formal Requirements Engineering process, reminder effectiveness, requirement traceability and explainability of AI decisions

# 4. Paper: A Novel Smart Study Plan for the Students
- **Paper link:** https://ijrpr.com/uploads/V6ISSUE4/IJRPR43785.pdf
- **Paper name:** A Novel Smart Study Plan for the Students 
- **Authors:** P. Avinash Varma, S.Sujatha, U. Rohan Krishna, M.Harini, S. Harsha Vardhan
- **Source:** International Journal of Research Publication and Reviews (https://ijrpr.com/)
- **Real problem:** University students often find it difficult to balance academics, assignments, hobbies and personal commitments
- **Why is this problem important:** Poor time management can cause missed deadlines, reduced productivity and stress
- **Gap:** They lacks detailed requirements engineering methodology, requirement validation process, AI recommendation transparency and adaptive reminder optimization based on user behavior
