# Research Gap

## Existing Research

Recent studies have examined the impact of AI and Large Language Models (LLMs) on software engineering and knowledge-intensive work.

Several studies focus on AI-supported Requirements Engineering activities, including requirements elicitation, analysis, validation, traceability, and documentation (Cheng et al., 2025; Fernandes et al., 2026; Vasudevan & Reddivari, 2025; Khan et al., 2024; Gustafsson, 2026).

Other studies investigate AI adoption in industrial software development and knowledge work, highlighting competencies such as AI literacy, prompt engineering, domain knowledge, and human–AI collaboration (Ronanki et al., 2026; Immonen, 2026).

Research has also examined labor market demand for AI-related skills through job advertisement analysis and has identified increasing demand for hybrid skill profiles that combine technical and soft skills (Nakash & Peretz, 2025). In addition, Kowalska-Styczeń and Juszczyk (2024) compared Business Analyst education with labor market requirements and identified a gap between academic preparation and industry expectations.

## Missing Research

Although existing studies discuss AI-related competencies, most research focuses on:

- Requirements Engineering professionals and activities.
- Product Management and other knowledge-intensive roles.
- General AI-related occupations.

There is limited research specifically examining Business Analysts working in software development environments where AI tools are being adopted.

Furthermore, no reviewed study directly compares the AI-related skills emphasized in academic literature with the skills requested in real-world Business Analyst job postings.

## Practical Problem

As organizations increasingly adopt AI tools, Business Analysts are expected to work with AI-supported processes and technologies.

However, it remains unclear:

- Which AI-related skills are consistently recommended by academic literature for Business Analysts.
- Whether these skills are reflected in current Business Analyst job postings.
- To what extent a gap exists between academic recommendations and industry expectations.

Understanding this gap can help educators, students, and practitioners better prepare for the changing role of Business Analysts in AI-enabled software development environments.
